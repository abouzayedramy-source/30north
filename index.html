Our Story

Where Coffee
Meets Culture

30 North was born from a single belief: that coffee should be more than a morning habit. It should be a ritual, an experience, a moment of pause in a relentless world.

We source our beans from the world's finest micro-farms — Ethiopian highlands, Colombian valleys, Yemeni terraces — and roast each batch to unlock its truest character. Every cup tells a story of origin, craft, and intention.
